## App name

BasePoint: Sari-Sari Store POS

## Short description

Sell, track stock and utang for your sari-sari store. Works offline.

## Full description

BasePoint is a simple point-of-sale app for sari-sari stores and other small shops. Ring up sales, keep track of stock and utang, and close the day with a cash count that adds up, all on your phone, with or without internet.

SELL FAST
• Tap a product to add it, or scan its barcode with the camera
• Popular items one tap away, a quantity keypad, and "repeat last sale"
• Hold a sale while you serve the next customer, then bring it back
• Sell something not in your list as a quick item, by price
• The cart stays put if you switch screens or the app closes

CHECKOUT AND CHANGE
• Cash, GCash, card, or any payment type you add
• Shows the change to give, down to the bills and coins
• Discounts with a reason, kept for your records
• Prints receipts on a Bluetooth thermal printer (58 mm or 80 mm), or share them as text

STOCK AND RESTOCK
• Products with photos, barcodes, prices and categories
• Low and out-of-stock alerts, with a suggested amount to buy
• A shopping list you can send to yourself, grouped by category
• Stock in a delivery for many products at once
• A stock history of every change: who made it and when

UTANG (CREDIT)
• Charge sales to a customer's tab and record payments
• Credit limits for the store and for each customer
• See who is overdue at a glance
• Send a ready-made SMS reminder from your own phone

CLOSE THE DAY
• Count the drawer by bills and coins; BasePoint shows if it is short or over
• A day-close report you can print or share
• Closed days with sales, short and over at a glance

REPORTS
• Sales for today, 7 days or 30 days
• Top products, sales by category, and products that are not selling
• Your busiest hours of the day

STAFF
• Each cashier signs in with their own PIN
• Sales, returns and day closes are recorded against whoever is signed in
• Managers approve closing the day, cash refunds, lowering stock and other sensitive actions

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account and no sign-up
• Everything is kept on your phone; nothing is sent to us
• Back up your store to Google Drive or any app you choose, and restore it on a new phone
• Works offline

In English and Filipino.
