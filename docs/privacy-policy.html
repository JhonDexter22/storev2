BasePoint

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Last updated 16 October 2026

In short

  • BasePoint is a point-of-sale app for small stores. It has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Everything you enter is kept on your phone. JHEDEV, the developer, does not receive, see or store your store's information, unless you send it to us yourself, for example an error log when asking for help.
  • Your information leaves the phone only when you send it: a backup you share, a receipt you print, an SMS reminder you send.
  • One exception happens automatically: the barcode scanner, built on Google ML Kit, sends Google basic technical diagnostics. It does not send camera images or the barcodes you scan. Details are below.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking of our own, and nothing is sold.

Who we are

BasePoint is made by JHEDEV. This policy covers the BasePoint app for Android (package name ph.jhedev.basepoint). Questions about it can be sent to basepoint.support2.0@gmail.com.

Information the app keeps on your phone

BasePoint stores what you and your staff enter, in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Store details and settings: store name, payment types, opening cash, credit limit, receipt printer choice, language and similar preferences.
  • Products: names, prices, categories, barcodes, stock counts and the product photos you take or choose.
  • Sales and money: sales, the items in them, payment method, discounts and their reasons, returns, closed days and cash counts, and a history of every stock change. Each of these records the name of the staff member who made it.
  • Customers who buy on credit (utang): the names and phone numbers you enter, their balances, charges, payments, and when a reminder was last sent.
  • Staff: names and roles. PINs are never stored as typed: each one is turned into a salted, repeatedly hashed code before it is saved.
  • An error log: when something goes wrong, the app records a short technical description of the error (for example, which screen it happened on). It stays on the phone, keeps at most 300 entries, and is only shared if you choose to share it. A shared error log also states the phone's screen size, text size and the app's language, to help find the cause.

Customer information belongs to your store. If you record customers' names and phone numbers, you are responsible for having their permission to do so.

BasePoint does not separately encrypt this information; it relies on Android's protection of each app's private storage and on your phone's screen lock. Keep your phone locked, and use BasePoint's staff PINs and Lock till so others cannot use the app in your name.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Backups. A backup is a file with your products, sales, customers and balances, closed days, stock history, settings, product photos and the error log. Staff names and PINs are not included. You choose where it goes through Android's share menu, for example Google Drive or a messaging app; that service then holds the copy under its own privacy policy. While being prepared, a copy is kept briefly in the app's temporary storage on the phone.
  • Things you share: receipts, a report summary, a restock shopping list or the error log, sent through Android's share menu to the app you pick.
  • SMS reminders and calls. A reminder opens your own messaging app with a message filled in (by default: the customer's name, your store's name and their balance). Nothing is sent unless you press send there, and your carrier's normal SMS charges apply. Calling a customer opens your phone's dialler.
  • Printing. Receipts and day-close slips are sent over Bluetooth to the receipt printer you choose.

Google ML Kit (barcode scanning)

BasePoint reads barcodes on the phone using Google ML Kit. The camera image and the barcode itself are processed on the phone and are not sent anywhere. ML Kit does, by Google's design, send Google technical information when the phone is online, which Google uses for diagnostics and usage analytics of ML Kit:

  • device information, such as manufacturer, model and Android version;
  • app information, such as the app's package name and version;
  • an identifier for this installation of the app;
  • performance figures, scanner settings (such as image format and resolution), input and output sizes, and error codes;
  • for the scanner's automatic zoom: a per-session identifier, zoom changes, and where in the frame a barcode was found.

Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and that ML Kit does not transfer it to third parties. JHEDEV does not receive it. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions the app asks for

CameraTo take product photos and scan barcodes, only while you are doing either.
Photos and filesTo choose a product photo from your gallery or a backup file to restore. On newer Android versions you pick the item yourself and the app sees only that item.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)To find and connect to your receipt printer. BasePoint does not use your location.
Internet and network statusRequired by Google ML Kit to send the diagnostics described above, and included by the receipt-printing component. BasePoint's own features work without an internet connection and do not send your store's information online.

What BasePoint does not do

  • It has no accounts and does not ask for your email or name to use it.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising libraries. JHEDEV does not use any analytics or tracking service; the only diagnostics sent anywhere are Google ML Kit's, described above.
  • It does not read your contacts; customers are typed in by you.
  • It does not access your location.
  • It does not sell or rent anyone's information.

Android backup and moving to a new phone

Android's automatic backup to your Google account is turned off for BasePoint, so Android does not copy the store's information there. To move to a new phone, make a BasePoint backup and restore it on the new phone. On Android 12 and newer, if you set up a new phone by copying directly from your old one, Android may also copy BasePoint's information straight from one phone to the other; it does not pass through JHEDEV.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Information stays on the phone until you delete it. Products can be deleted one at a time. Sales, customers and their balances stay as your store's records. Staff removed from the roster keep their name on the sales and closed days they recorded.
  • Settings → Clear all data (it asks for the manager PIN) deletes all products, stock history, sales, returns, closed days, customers and their balances. Staff and settings are kept, and product photo files stay in the app's private storage until the app is uninstalled.
  • Before a restore replaces the store's information, BasePoint saves a copy of the replaced information in the app's private storage as a safety net.
  • The error log can be cleared from Settings → Help → Error log.
  • Uninstalling BasePoint deletes everything it stored on the phone, including photos and safety copies.
  • Backups and other files you have sent elsewhere are not affected; delete them where you sent them.

Children

BasePoint is a business tool for store owners and their staff. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If BasePoint changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed before the new version of the app is released.
